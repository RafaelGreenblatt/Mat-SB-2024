## Esercizio

Si calcolino i seguenti integrali:

a. $\int\left(\sqrt x - \frac1{\sqrt x} \right) \, d x$
b. $\int\left(e^{2x} - \frac12 e^{x/2} \right) \, d x$
c. $\int \frac{dx}{2x}$
d. $\int \frac{t-1}{t^2} d t$
e. $\int_0^\pi (\sin x - \cos x) d x$
f. $\int_0^{\pi/2} (y - \sin y) d y$
